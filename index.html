TACTIC

Toolkit for Automated Compliance and Technology-Integrated Control

KU Leuven project 3H260290 · 1 October 2026 – 30 September 2030 · Funded by the KU Leuven Special Research Fund (BOF), ID-N programme (IDN/26/001)

Summary

The rapid growth of artificial intelligence (AI), data-driven technologies and digital infrastructures is transforming societies and economies, creating both opportunities but also complex regulatory challenges. In the European Union (EU), for instance, a wave of new legislation with regard to data and AI has increased compliance demands and costs.
Automated compliance, which refers to using technology to streamline adherence to applicable legal frameworks, offers a promising solution by improving efficiency, accuracy and consistency while reducing compliance costs. However, previous attempts to develop automated compliance solutions were not successful because technology overshadowed legal and governance considerations.
TACTIC (Toolkit for Automated Compliance and Technology-Integrated Control) will address this gap by developing a holistic framework aligning the legal, technical and governance dimensions of automated compliance. Using the EU as a case study, TACTIC will provide tools and recommendations to ensure responsible, transparent and scalable automated compliance solutions, with globally applicable insights on data and AI governance.
